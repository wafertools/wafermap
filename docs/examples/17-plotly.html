§17 — Plotly compatibility buildScene · toPlotly · Plotly.react · getDieAtPoint · onSceneOptionsChange sync
The canvas renderer (renderWaferMap) is the preferred surface. The Plotly SVG renderer (toPlotly) is here for compatibility with existing dashboards and SVG export workflows. Change any toolbar control on the left — the Plotly chart on the right re-renders to match via onSceneOptionsChange. Click a die in the Plotly chart — getDieAtPoint maps the event back to the matching Die object.
