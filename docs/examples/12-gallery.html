§12 — Building a lot gallery renderWaferGallery · shared controls · stacked modes · click ↗ to open modal
renderWaferGallery(container, items) renders a lot of wafers in a responsive card grid with shared controls. All cards update together when you change mode, colour, rotation, or flip in the shared toolbar. Switch to a stacked mode (toolbar mode picker) to aggregate the lot — one card per bin or test parameter. Click the ↗ expand icon on any card to open it as a full interactive map.
