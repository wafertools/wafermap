1. renderWaferMap — no maxSize

Baseline, unchanged behaviour: fills the container's full width. Expected: width = container width.

2. renderWaferMap — maxSize: 300

Expected: exactly 300×300, flush to the container's top-left, toolbar in its corner (not the container's).

3. renderWaferMap — maxSize: 200, interactive

Deliberately a different cap from case 2 so the two aren't confusable. Click into it and press E (or use the toolbar expand button): the modal should open the map at full size, escaping the 200px cap. Closing it should snap back to 200.

4. renderWaferGallery — 1 item, no maxSize

The originally reported bug. Previously stretched to full container width and overflowed vertically; expected now: capped at the 480px density-derived default, not stretched.

5. renderWaferGallery — 1 item, maxSize: 250

Explicit hard cap. Expected: 250×250 card at the top-left.

6. renderWaferGallery — 3 items, maxSize: 250

Expected: three 250px cards packed adjacent from the left (12px gaps only) — no wide whitespace bands between columns.

7. renderWaferGallery — 300mm wafer, 3mm pitch (~7.9k DPW), no maxSize

Mid-density. The cap is derived, not a fixed step: 300mm ÷ 3mm needs 300×(4/3)+124 = 524px to keep dies at 4px, so it should land exactly there — above the 480 default, below the 720 ceiling.

8. renderWaferGallery — 300mm wafer, 2mm pitch (~17.7k DPW), no maxSize

High density. This one needs 724px, which exceeds the ceiling — expected to clamp at 720px and let dies shrink rather than growing without bound.
